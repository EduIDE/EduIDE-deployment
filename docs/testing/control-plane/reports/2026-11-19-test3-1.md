## Summary

- test3 on `sha-2bb3ecc` and later is fit for further testing with students' real launches: of 178 planned tests 128 pass, 13 fail, 11 are blocked, 9 skipped and 17 have no result in the notes (browser modules 02 and 08 are only partly done).
- The 13 failures: nine belong to findings fixed in batch 1 or 2 but not re-run (OPR-07, 08, 11, 12, POOL-06, 08, 13, SEC-11, MON-11); MON-01 and MON-02 were a deployment gap (monitoring was enabled later, the alert EduIDEAuthzDown fired) and were not formally re-run; MON-08 (cluster certificate alerts) and IDE-05 (clone into an empty workspace, fix in EduIDE#238 not re-tested) are open.
- The security basics hold: no anonymous path reaches a session, pods and agents are isolated, the instance token is required, authz fails closed (500) with the owner cookie, no launch data was found in any log, status, span or metric.
- The most important defects of the first pass were operational rather than functional: no logs on the happy path, no egress policy, ten minutes of "Starting" for a broken image, idle pods never rolled after a template change, and an agent restart that silently lost the injected data. All are fixed; the agent re-claim and the new logs and events were verified in the disruptive run.
- Open: the certificate alerts of the cluster do not cover test3 (F11), the webview host negotiates only HTTP/1.1 (F7), the notice replay after an IDE restart (F26), the bare 503 during a service outage (F32), and a re-run of module 10, the fixed failures and the browser halves is still needed.
